# deprecations
 - Deprecated: `rocm.n_max_profiling_configs`. Instead, use ck-tile based configs `rocm.ck_max_profiling_configs` and `rocm.ck_tile_max_profiling_configs` ([#152341](https://github.com/pytorch/pytorch/pull/152341))
